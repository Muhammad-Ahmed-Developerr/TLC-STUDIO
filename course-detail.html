VTCT ACCREDITED DIPLOMA

Level 2 Hair Professional

Build the essential technical skills, client communication confidence, and hairdressing theory required to thrive in premier salons across the UK.

18 Months Duration Hybrid Study Mode Ofqual Level 2 Funding Available

Course Overview

The Level 2 Hair Professional Diploma is the industry-recognized qualification for aspiring hairdressers. Delivered through TLC Academy’s hybrid model, you will master theoretical principles online through our portal and TLC AI assistant, while completing intensive practical workshops in our accredited salon campuses.

Curriculum Modules

Module 1: Professional Client Consultation & Scalp Analysis

Learn trichology fundamentals, porosity testing, scalp diagnosis, client communication protocols, and recording legal treatment cards.

Module 2: Shampooing, Conditioning & Steam Hydration

Master scalp massage techniques, conditioning formulations for Type 1 to Type 4 hair textures, and thermal steam treatments.

Module 3: Precision Cutting Techniques

One-length cuts, uniform layers, graduation, fringe cutting, cross-checking, and scissor-over-comb techniques.

Module 4: Styling, Finishing & Silk Pressing

Blow-drying, iron curling, silk pressing, thermal protection, and long hair updo styling.

Practical Assessment & Qualification

Assessment is conducted via practical observations on live client models in our academy salon, portfolio evidence submission, and digital multiple-choice theory exams.
